Imubit / AI platform / ML workflows

Designing Imubit’s AI/ML platform from zero

As Imubit’s first and sole product designer, I designed the product platform from the ground up — from navigation and core modules to model building, evaluation, monitoring, deployment workflows, data management, on-prem improvements, and the design system behind them.

Challenge

Industrial ML workflows are powerful, but hard to scale when they depend on experts, fragmented internal tools, and tacit knowledge.

Approach

Structure the platform into clear product areas: data import and management, model building, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, reuse, permissions, and collaboration.

Value

Enable domain experts and delivery teams to work in a low-code/no-code AI environment, while still supporting advanced AI users through permissions.

Role and ownership

I was responsible for the UX/UI of the platform and its product language. During the Model Builder initiative, I also carried a Product Manager role for almost a year, taking the product from preliminary definition and hands-on design through development and deployment before a dedicated PM joined.

First / sole designerDesigned the platform from zero and owned the product design direction.
Design system from scratchCreated a reusable component library and interaction language that accelerated development and improved consistency.
Product design + PM overlapLed hands-on UX/UI while also managing product decisions, prioritization, and stakeholder alignment for Model Builder.
Navigation & orientation

From tools to platform.

The platform needed to feel like one coherent environment rather than a collection of separate internal tools.

Model Builder

Models as managed objects.

Model creation and management became a product workflow with ownership, status, permissions, filtering, and action flows.

Data preparation

Turning expertise into logic.

Domain experts need to identify shutdowns, outliers, bad periods, and irrelevant operating states before training.

Reusable logic safely

Reusable worksheet concept.

Data-preparation logic was designed to become reusable knowledge rather than one-off setup, helping teams apply trusted rules consistently across models and reduce repeated manual work.

Trust before deployment

Evaluation as confidence building.

Before deployment, users need to understand whether the model behaves as expected. What-if interactions let them change one variable and see whether prediction response makes domain sense.

AI-assisted workflow

From static mockups to working prototypes.

AI-assisted prototyping helped explore complex flows, states, and redesign directions faster and with more realistic interaction behavior.

Operational visibility

Monitoring deployed value.

Production-facing views were designed to help teams understand model status, operational performance, and where attention was needed — while keeping complex industrial AI workflows readable and actionable.

Impact & decisions

From service-heavy delivery to a scalable product platform.

The platform was designed to democratize industrial AI: giving domain experts, delivery teams, and customers a modern way to configure, evaluate, trust, and monitor models without relying on engineers for every step.

Platform impact

Main workflow for the ML lifecycle

Model Builder became the flagship expression of the platform: a low-code/no-code environment for building, evaluating, iterating, deploying, and monitoring industrial AI/ML models. It replaced older editor tools and supported Imubit’s shift from service-heavy delivery toward a scalable product platform.

Democratizing AI

Less dependency on specialists

The guided workflow reduced reliance on engineering and AI specialists, while permission-based advanced screens still allowed expert users to add custom code, weights, and deeper configuration when needed.

Reusable logic

Faster, safer configuration

Repositories for data rules, calculated tags, and configuration patterns reduced repetitive setup work, minimized copy-paste errors, and created more reliable model-building practices across workspaces.

Trust & explainability

Cutting through the black box

Interactive evaluation let users change tag values and immediately see prediction behavior. This helped domain experts compare model response with physical-process knowledge and build confidence before deployment.

Contextual collaboration

Notes inside the model

Model Notes moved discussion from screenshots and external tools into the model context itself: saved scenarios, grades, text discussions, replies, mentions, and email notifications tied directly to the relevant model state.

Impact

The work helped shift complex ML activity into a repeatable, trusted product workflow: data import, data management, model creation, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, reuse, collaboration, and ongoing improvement. It became a strategic platform asset for customers, internal teams, leadership, and investors.
