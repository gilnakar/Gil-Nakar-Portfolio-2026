Design system v1.5

Markiply design system

Tokens and components used by the MVP. Everything here is in mk.css. Nothing is under 13px (one exception: 12px metadata on My decisions) and every text pair meets WCAG AA. White ground, soft lilac washes from the v6 palette, the logo colors in one thin line at the top, and the gradient only on the one primary action. One type scale, three Inter weights. Full rules are in DESIGN-SYSTEM.md.
